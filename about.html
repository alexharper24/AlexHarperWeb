The person behind the work.

Harper Studio is small on purpose. Your site is designed, built, and managed end to end, so it is never handed off to someone who has not seen it before.

Hi, I'm Alex Harper.

Growing up in a Christian and military household, discipline, structure, and organization were natural characteristics I observed and absorbed into who I am today. Those traits have shaped the way I work, influencing me to plan before I build, maintain order, and notice the small things that are easy to miss.

My professional career in IT has taught me that everyone has a job to do, with their own skills and gifts to bring to it. My part is handling the technical work that businesses, churches, and nonprofits simply expect to run in the background, so they can stay focused on the valuable work only they can produce. Taking that weight off them, and providing real value in its place, is what I enjoy most.

What matters most to me is finishing work I'm proud to put my name on. Not the least amount of effort, but the extra pass that makes it right. This allows the look and feel to transition smoothly, the message be delivered clearly for the purpose intended, and the final touches to hold up for performance and consistency across every platform.

Based in New Albany, Indiana, working with organizations wherever they are.

Alex Harper, Founder
