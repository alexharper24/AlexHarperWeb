Five hero treatments, built and running

Each stage below is the real header, the real photo, and the real stylesheet, with only the layout changed. Resize the window to see how each behaves on a phone. Nothing here is wired into the homepage. Where the supplied mockup had a problem I have built the fixed version and said so.

Work neededNone. This is what ships today.
PhotoUsed as shot, full composition, never cropped.
ContrastNavy on cream, passes comfortably.

Editorial split

Work neededGrid split, photo re-anchored to its subject.
PhotoNeeds the right ~56%. The existing mobile crop is already exactly that.
Fixed vs mockupThe mockup runs the nav across the photo and three links become unreadable. Header kept solid here.

Floating content panel

PhotoSuits this one best: the panel covers the flat cream field, not the laptop.
WatchA card over a photo can read as a cookie banner. Radius, shadow and grid alignment are what stop it.
