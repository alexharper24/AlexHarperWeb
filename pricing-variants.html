Three ways to distinguish the build and care groups by colour

Same content in each, pulled from the live homepage. All three restore a larger lane title than what is currently live, since making them quieter was the wrong call. Pick one and I will ship it; this file gets deleted.

Option A

Tinted panels

Each group sits in its own soft panel: warm cream for the one-time build, a cooler near-white for the ongoing care plan. Containment does the work, so the section reads as one thing holding two groups. Safest of the three.

Option B

Navy care lane

The build stays warm cream; the care plan becomes a navy panel using the band colour already on the site. Strongest separation by far, it makes the white cards and the red Most-popular flag pop, and it gives the recurring plan the weight it deserves as the thing that actually carries the business.

Accent rail and filled label
