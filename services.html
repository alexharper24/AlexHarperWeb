What I do

Six things, done properly.

Not a menu of add-ons. These are the pieces that make a small organization's site work.

Custom design

Every site starts with your work, not a template. I design around who you serve and what they need to find, so the layout, wording, and imagery all point the same direction.

Redesigns & refreshes

An aged site rarely needs starting from nothing. I rebuild what's there so it looks current and works properly on phones, keeping the pages and search history that already work.

Fast, modern build

Hand-built rather than assembled from heavy page builders, so pages stay fast and easy to maintain. Every page is checked on real phones, not just resized in a browser.

Hosting & maintenance

I host the sites I build, so there's no finger-pointing when something needs attention. That covers the server, the certificate, backups, and monitoring. You never log into a control panel.

Get found locally

Your Google Business Profile claimed and set up properly, plus the search basics done right at launch: real titles and descriptions, structured data so Google and AI assistants understand who you are, a sitemap, and fast load times. Managed month to month on Standard and Premium.

Ongoing updates

New photos, seasonal hours, an event, a staff change, a whole new page. Email or text me the change and I handle it, usually within a few business days.
