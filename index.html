How it feels

Calm, written down, and easier than you expect.

The process is intentionally plain. We define what matters, I build in public enough for you to react, then the launch details get checked before anyone is pointed at the site.

Read the written commitments →
  1. 1Clarify the job. We talk through goals, pages, audience, and what your current setup needs.
  2. 2Agree on the shape. You get scope, an expected timeline, and typical pricing before work starts.
  3. 3Build with checkpoints. You see the site take shape and can respond before anything goes live.
  4. 4Launch and care for it. Forms, domain, speed, mobile, and backups are checked, then care begins if you want it.
The promise: if something I built breaks, I fix it. Scope or price changes do not move forward without your approval. The site stays portable.
