Good to know

Questions people ask before they call.

What does a website cost?

Builds run $800 for one page, $1,600 for up to six, and from $2,400 with no page limit. Care plans are $65 to $325 a month, with a reduced rate for churches and nonprofits. The plans page lists every number, what each tier includes, and what it does not.

Who owns the site, the domain, and the content?

You do, from day one, all registered in your name. Nothing here locks you in, and renewals pass through at cost.

What if I want to leave later?

Plans are month to month with 30 days notice and no contract. Since everything is already yours, leaving is just a handover of logins.

Do I actually need a new website?

Maybe not, and I'll say so. Send me your web address and I'll review it for free: what's working, what's broken on phones, and whether it's worth fixing.
