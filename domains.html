What is included

The fiddly details, taken off your plate.

Domain registration

I register or transfer your domain and keep it renewed, so it never quietly lapses. It stays in your name.

DNS management

I set up and maintain your DNS records so your site and email point where they should.

Redirects & records

Old links, www vs non-www, and any records you need, handled cleanly so nothing quietly breaks.

Kept online for you

If something changes, I handle it. You should never have to log into a registrar or wonder why the site is down.

Whose account it lives in

The cleanest setup is a domain account you own, with delegated access for me to manage the technical records. If I manage the registrar account for you, you are still listed as the registrant and can transfer the domain out. Either way, the domain is yours and it leaves with you.

A note on email

I manage the DNS records your email depends on, so your mail keeps flowing when the site changes or moves. Standing up brand-new email accounts is separate work, and I am glad to point you in the right direction if you need it.
