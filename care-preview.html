Care plans: what the mockup actually changes.

Worth saying first, because it narrows the question: the live section is already navy with cream cards and a Most popular ribbon, and every figure in the mockup already exists on the site unchanged, including the reduced church and nonprofit rates and the December 31, 2026 date. So this is not a recolour. Five specific things differ.

  • Circled check marks on every bullet, where the live list is plain.
  • An icon on each of the two callouts.
  • The callout label runs into its sentence instead of sitting above it as an eyebrow, and the offer copy is cut to a single line.
  • Three bullets a card instead of four.
  • No link through to each tier's own page.

The first three are straight improvements. The last two cost real content, so they are shown separately rather than bundled in.

A · live today

Plain bullets, stacked callouts

Exactly what plans.html renders now. Four bullets a card, a link through to every tier page, and the two callouts carrying an eyebrow, a heading, and the full offer paragraph.
