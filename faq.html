Pricing and plans

Cost, plans, and what you are paying for.

What does a website cost?

Builds come four ways, priced by how much site you need: Starter is typically $800 for one focused page, Complete is typically $1,600 for up to six pages, Signature starts around $2,400 for larger custom sites, and Advanced is quoted to scope for ordering, donations, member areas, or other custom functionality.

Every build includes domain and DNS coordination, a working contact form, email pointed correctly, mobile-first testing, basic search setup, and a clear export path if you ever need to host the site elsewhere. Monthly care starts at $65/mo, with reduced rates for churches and nonprofits. Full details are on the plans page.

Why do you publish starting prices when most designers make me ask?

Because you should not need a sales call just to decide whether the conversation is worth having. The published numbers are normal starting points. If a project has unusual content, access issues, integrations, or scope, I tell you before anything starts.

Do you work with churches and nonprofits?

Yes. A large part of my work is churches and nonprofits, and reduced care pricing is available: Essential $45/mo, Standard $100/mo, and Premium from $225/mo. Same work, same attention, same care.

Builds and scope

What is included, what is extra, and how long it usually takes.

What counts as an edit, a request, or a revision round?

A revision round is one consolidated pass during a build: you review the design and send your changes together. I make them, then show you the result. Starter includes one round; Complete and Signature include two.

An edit on Essential is a small change to something already on your site: a photo, hours, price, or paragraph. A request on Standard can be a little bigger: a staff member, menu section, event, or photo batch.

A brand-new page, redesign, new functionality, or bulk work across the whole site is a project. I flag that before I start and quote it separately.

What is not included in a build?

Writing all content from scratch, professional photography, a new logo or full brand identity, paid advertising, ongoing SEO campaigns, and third-party subscriptions are quoted separately.

That does not mean they are off the table. It just means the build price does not hide them. I do shape, structure, and proof the words and photos you send, and third-party costs always pass through at cost.

How long will my website take?

A one-page Starter can be ready as soon as 1 week when content, approvals, access, and third-party pieces are ready. Complete usually runs two to three weeks. Signature commonly takes two to four. The final launch window depends on content, approvals, access to accounts, and any third-party pieces.

Care after launch

What happens once the site is live.

Do I have to be on a care plan?

The Starter build is paired with a care plan because that price only works when hosting and upkeep stay simple. Complete, Signature, and Advanced builds are yours to run however you like. Most clients stay on care because hosting, security, backups, and updates are handled in one place.

Do you handle my Google Business Profile?

Yes, when local search is part of the scope or you are on a care plan that includes it. It is not automatically included in every Starter build.

Setup can include categories and services, hours and contact details, real photos, duplicate listings if they exist, and consistency with the website.

Ongoing management is part of Standard and Premium care. I keep details accurate as they change, watch for suggested edits, draft review replies for you to approve, and send a plain monthly note on what happened. I do not guarantee rankings, buy reviews, or write fake reviews.

What does your guarantee cover?

Anything I built that breaks gets fixed free, nothing launches without real-device testing, scope and expected pricing are written down before work starts, the site stays portable, and third-party costs pass through at cost. Read the whole thing on the guarantee page.

Ownership and fit

What stays yours, and whether this is the right move.

Who owns the website when it is done?

You do, with one practical hosting distinction. I usually manage hosting for the sites I care for, but the site is not trapped there. Your domain, DNS, and content stay clearly yours, and I can export the site files if you need to host elsewhere.

What happens if we part ways?

Care plans are month to month with 30 days notice and no contract. If you leave, I export the site code and hand over the domain, DNS, files, and anything registered to you so you can host it elsewhere.

Do I actually need a new website?

Not always. If your current site is accurate, loads quickly, and works properly on a phone, you may not need me yet, and I will tell you so.

If you are not sure, send me your web address. I will look it over and reply with what is working, what is not, and whether anything is worth fixing.
