Case study

A breeder since 1989, off Wix and posting its own puppies.

Teapup raises puppies in Syracuse, Indiana, and the Internet Archive has saved its website since 2006. The site ran on Weebly until December 2023 and on Wix from January 2024, and both times the puppies lived inside a page builder. The new site went live on November 7, 2026, with a page for every puppy, litter and adult dog and an editor the owners use to post puppies themselves.

What the archive shows

The site a family found before.

Every point below comes from the Internet Archive's saved copies of the old site, not from memory. The figures come from the May 19, 2026 capture of the homepage.

  1. Two platforms in three years. The archive shows the site on Weebly until December 2023 and on Wix from January 2024, so the business had already rebuilt once.
  2. A heavy first download. The homepage alone was 863,500 bytes of HTML with 57 scripts, before any photo started to load.
  3. A description search engines cut short. The homepage description ran 393 characters, and a search result shows roughly the first 155, so most of it never appeared.
  4. No preview for a shared link. The homepage had no social image tag, so a link pasted into Facebook or a text message showed whatever the app guessed.

Built around the family choosing a puppy

What the new site does.

A family choosing a puppy wants to see the puppy, its parents and the price, and to know what happens next. Every page works toward that.

  1. A page for every puppy, litter and adult dog. Each puppy page carries its photos, its parents, its price and how to reserve it.
  2. The owners post puppies themselves. An editor behind Google sign-in lets the family add puppies and litters and change their status, and the site rebuilds itself when they publish.
  3. Breed guides answer the first questions. Each breed has a guide covering size, shedding, colors and price, linked from the puppies page.
  4. The guarantee, word for word. The health guarantee page carries Teapup's guarantee exactly as it is printed on the receipt that goes home with every puppy.
  5. Every old link still lands. 135 redirect rules send old Wix addresses to the matching new page, so links in search results and old posts keep working.
Where it stands. The site went live on November 7, 2026 at teapup.com, with 265 pages in its sitemap. The family posts new puppies and litters through the editor, and each publish updates the live site.
