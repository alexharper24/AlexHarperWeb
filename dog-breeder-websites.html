Dog breeder website design

What a breeder site needs to do well.

A family deciding where to get a puppy is making a big decision. These are the pieces that help them make it with you.

A page for every puppy

Photos and details for each puppy, with whether they are still available, so families are not left guessing.

Parents and litters

The dams and sires behind your puppies, so families can see where their puppy comes from.

An application and waitlist

Families apply and join your waitlist online, and their answers arrive in your inbox ready to read.

Your process in writing

How it works from first contact to go-home day, with your health guarantee and agreement published for families to read first.

Breed guides families search for

Pages that answer the questions families type into Google, such as size, colors, shedding, and price, and bring those searches to you.

Your home address kept private

The site and your Google Business Profile show a service area, never where you live.

Common questions

What breeders usually ask first.

Can I update my own puppy listings?

Yes, if you want to. An editor can be built in so you add litters, post photos, and mark puppies reserved yourself, and it is quoted as part of the build. If you would rather send changes to me, that works too.

Will my home address show on the site?

No. A home-based program shows a service area on the website and on its Google Business Profile, never the street address.

Can families pay a deposit online?

It is possible if you are interested. We work out the right setup for your program, and the deposit terms you already use stay in your words.
