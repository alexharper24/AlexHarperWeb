Case study

Labs, Goldens and Berners from Syracuse, Indiana, on a site the family updates itself.

Sweet Puppy Paws has raised Labrador Retrievers, Golden Retrievers and Bernese Mountain Dogs in Syracuse, Indiana since 2018. Its Wix site showed the puppies as a gallery of photos, and its pages told search engines little about the business. The new site went live on November 7, 2026, with a page for each puppy, litter and breed and an editor the owners use for updates.

What the archive shows

The site a family found before.

Every point below comes from the Internet Archive's saved copies of the old site, not from memory. The figures come from the July 13, 2026 capture of the homepage.

  1. Puppies as a photo gallery. The puppies page opened on a sliding gallery, without a breed, a price or a date in the first screen.
  2. A heavy first download. The homepage alone was 815,072 bytes of HTML with 58 scripts, before any photo started to load.
  3. Little for search engines to read. The only structured data was a basic website entry, with nothing to say this is a breeder, where it is or how to reach it.
  4. A description search engines cut short. The homepage description ran 358 characters, and a search result shows roughly the first 155, so most of it never appeared.

Built around the family choosing a puppy

What the new site does.

A family looking at a Lab, a Golden or a Berner wants to see the puppy, know when it can come home and understand the guarantee. Every page works toward that.

  1. A page for each puppy, litter and breed. Every puppy has its own page with photos, a price and a go-home date, and each litter and breed has a page of its own.
  2. Breed guides answer the first questions. Each breed has a guide covering size, shedding, colors and price, linked from the puppies page.
  3. The owners update it themselves. An editor behind Google sign-in lets the family add puppies and litters and change their status, and each publish updates the live site.
  4. The guarantee, word for word. The health guarantee page carries the guarantee exactly as it appears on the agreement each family signs when their puppy goes home.
  5. Every old link still lands. 52 redirect rules send old Wix addresses to the matching new page, so links in search results and old posts keep working.
Where it stands. The site went live on November 7, 2026 at sweetpuppypaws.net, with 160 pages in its sitemap. The family posts new puppies and litters through the editor.
