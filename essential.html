How requests work

Just tell me what you need.

No tickets and no portal to log into. Email or text me what you want changed. I confirm I have it and tell you when to expect it done.

Essential includes four edits a year. An edit is a change to something already on your site: a new photo, updated hours or prices, a corrected typo, a reworded paragraph. Roughly half an hour of work or less. If what you ask for is bigger than that, I tell you before I start and give you a flat quote, so you are never billed for something you did not agree to.

Good to know

The practical bits.

  • Billed monthly, in advance. Cancel anytime with 30 days notice.
  • Your domain and content are always yours. The domain sits in my registrar account with you as the registrant, or in your own account with delegated access to me. Your choice, and either way you could recover it without me.
  • Hosting runs on trusted platforms, so uptime depends partly on those providers.
  • Any third-party costs, such as domain renewals or premium services, are passed through at cost, never marked up.
  • If you ever leave, I hand everything over cleanly: the domain, every site file, and anything registered to you, with no hostage-taking.
  • Full terms are shared before anything starts, so nothing is a surprise.
