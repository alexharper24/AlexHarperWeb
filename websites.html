How long it takes

A one-page Starter launches about two weeks after your content is ready, Complete runs two to three weeks, and Signature two to four. You get a firm date in writing with your quote.

What a revision round means

You review the design and send all your changes together; I make them and show you the result. That is one round. Starter includes one, Complete and Signature include two. It is not one change per round, and it is not unlimited back and forth, which is what keeps a project actually launching. Full definitions are on the plans page.

After launch

Once your site is live, a care plan keeps it hosted, secure, and up to date, so you never have to think about it.
