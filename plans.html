Plain definitions

What the pricing words mean.

These definitions keep the quote clear and prevent surprise bills later.

A revision round

You review the design and send all your changes together. I make them, then show you the result. That is one round.

It is not one change per round, and it is not unlimited back and forth. If you need more after the included rounds, I tell you before any extra work starts.

An edit Essential plan

A change to something already on your site: swapping a photo, updating hours or prices, fixing a typo, or rewording a paragraph. Roughly half an hour or less.

A request Standard plan

Anything an edit covers, and a little bigger: a staff member, menu section, event, photo batch, or seasonal change. Up to about an hour of work.

Not an edit or a request

A brand-new page, redesign, new functionality, or bulk work across the whole site. If a request is really a project, I quote it before I start.

Quoted separately

What a build price does not hide.

  • Writing all content from scratch. I shape and proof what you send; original copywriting is its own project.
  • Professional photography. I place and optimize what you have and will tell you if a shoot is worth it.
  • Logo or full brand identity. Cleaning up an existing mark is different from designing a new one.
  • Paid advertising or SEO campaigns. Your site is built to be found; ongoing campaigns are separate.
  • Third-party subscriptions. Domains and tools are billed by providers at their price, never marked up.
