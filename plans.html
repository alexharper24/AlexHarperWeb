Plain definitions

What these words actually mean.

Terms like "revision round" get used loosely across this industry. Here is exactly what they mean here.

A revision round
You review the design and send me all your changes together. I make them, then show you the result. That is one round.
It is not one change per round, and it is not unlimited back and forth. Rounds keep the project moving so it actually launches. If you need more after they are used, they are billed at a flat hourly rate you approve first, and I will always tell you before we get there.
An edit Essential plan
A change to something already on your site: swapping a photo, updating your hours or prices, fixing a typo, rewording a paragraph. Roughly half an hour or less of work. Essential covers four a year.
A request Standard plan
Anything an edit covers, and bigger: a new staff member, a new menu section, an event, a batch of photos, a seasonal change. Up to about an hour of work. Standard covers four a month.
Unused requests do not roll over to the next month. Send several at once and I will count them fairly, not pedantically.
Not an edit or a request
A brand-new page, a redesign, new functionality, or bulk work across the whole site. On Premium there is room in the plan for genuinely new pages. On any plan, if what you ask for is really a project, I tell you before I start and give you a flat quote. You are never billed for something you did not agree to.
