Work I stand behind, in writing.

"Care in every detail" is easy to say. Here is what it means when something goes wrong, when a quote is on the table, and when you decide to leave.

The guarantee

Six commitments, no fine print.

If something I built breaks, I fix it. Free.

Workmanship is on me, for as long as I manage the site. A broken page, a form that stopped delivering, a layout that fell apart on a new phone: fixed at no charge, not billed as a change request.

Nothing launches untested.

Every page is checked on real devices before it goes live: desktop, tablet, and the phones most of your visitors actually use.

The quote holds.

You get scope, price, and timeline in writing before work begins. The price does not move unless the scope does, and you approve any change first.

You own everything.

Your domain, hosting, and content are registered in your name from day one. There is no proprietary platform underneath and nothing to buy back later.

Leaving is easy, on purpose.

Care plans are month to month with 30 days notice and no contract. Because everything is already yours, leaving is a handover of logins, not a negotiation.

Costs pass through at cost.

Domain renewals and other third-party costs are billed at exactly what they cost, never marked up. You can check every one against the provider's own price list.

Why I can offer this

Hand-built sites have very little that can quietly rot: no plugins to conflict, no platform updates to break things overnight. Standing behind the work is cheap when the work is built carefully in the first place. That is the point.

Hold me to it.

Tell me what you're working on and you'll get a flat quote, a real timeline, and these commitments behind both.
